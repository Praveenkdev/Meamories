To Kitty 🐱❤️

✦ ━━━━━ ⚜ ━━━━━ ✦

First, I'm sorry for saying all this to you. I don't know how you'll take this or what you'll think after reading it, but please don't get tense or angry immediately, okay? Just listen to what I have to say first. And then, whatever you feel is completely valid. There's nothing wrong with being angry or upset.

First, I believe you might remember something I told you before. I once told you that I liked a girl one-sidedly. I hope you remember that conversation. Well, this message is actually meant for that girl. I think by now you might have understood who I'm talking about. ❤️

I've been noticing you since our first year, literally from the very first day. I still remember you wearing that purple dress (I think it was purple 😅). From that day onwards, something about you caught my attention, and eventually, I genuinely fell for you.

I tried many times to talk to you, but I couldn't gather the courage. I was always afraid of approaching you because I didn't know how you would respond or what you would think about me. Honestly, I was scared of what your opinion of me might be.

Whenever I came near your classroom, 6003, I think you might remember that you would usually be with your three friends. I mostly got to see you during lunch breaks, and sometimes while wandering around outside the classrooms. From the day I first saw you, I've always felt a strange sensation in my heart whenever I saw you. And the funny thing is, even today, that feeling hasn't changed. It's still the same. 🥹❤️

It was only during our second year that I finally started talking to you. Even now, I haven't forgotten those initial conversations. From the very first time we started talking until today, I've always felt genuinely happy whenever I got the chance to speak with you.

Sometimes, I still wish I could be in your class just to spend more time with you. 😅

As I got to know you better, the way I thought about you completely changed from what I had initially imagined. I started discovering more about your personality, your childish side, your silly behaviours and all those little things that make you who you are.

We also had some fights during the same year. There were situations where, because of me, you had arguments with the boys in our class, and eventually, you refused to talk to me. That genuinely hurt me, but more importantly, I realised that my behaviour had hurt you too.

I was just playing around with you, but I didn't understand that it was hurting your feelings. I'm genuinely sorry for that. I know I should have been more careful with your feelings.

As time passed, I got to know you even better. Your childish side is honestly one of the cutest things about you. 😅❤️ Sometimes, I wish I could preserve that side of yours forever. The silly things you do, the way you behave sometimes and even the way you get angry are all little things I've come to appreciate.

And whenever you get angry, I know there is usually a reason behind it. Sometimes, even when I don't understand it immediately, I realise later that you had a point.

The decisions and advice you've given me have saved me many times. Sometimes, I genuinely wish I could always be by your side, supporting you whenever you need someone. But I also understand that it might not always be possible.

And honestly, when I think about why I like you so much, I don't even have one particular reason. I just do. Maybe it's all those little things about you that made me feel this way. ❤️

When you went abroad for your research studies, I started thinking about you even more. I used to ask our mutual friends when you would come back. They even used to scold me, saying, "Engala la unakku theriyalaya? Unakku ava mattum dhaan theriyuma?" 😂

Of course, I knew! But what could I do? I genuinely wanted to talk to you.

I seriously thought about calling you, but I didn't know how to approach you. I was worried that you might feel uncomfortable if I suddenly called you. So I decided to wait until you came back and let you talk whenever you felt like it.

I missed you a lot. And whenever I told you that I missed you, I genuinely meant every word. I don't know how you interpreted those words or whether you misunderstood me sometimes, but I never wanted to make you uncomfortable.

Then, when you finally came back to India, I was genuinely so happy! I had been waiting to see you and talk to you again. I was honestly so excited about it. It felt like I had something wonderful to look forward to. 🥹

After that, I also got opportunities to spend time with you. Sometimes, when I had projects or other work to do, I would come to the same classroom, sit quietly and just enjoy being around you. Maybe you wouldn't even have noticed, but those little moments meant a lot to me.

I also shared many of my personal problems with you. I've cried in front of you and shared things that I haven't completely shared with anyone else. There are other people I've talked to, of course, but the way I've opened up to you is different.

You've been there for me during some of my lowest moments, and that means more to me than I can explain.

Especially that day at Lava, when I was crying about an issue and sharing everything with you. I will never forget that moment because you were there, comforting me when I needed someone the most.

There are so many moments like that in my life. So many happy memories that I genuinely cherish because you were a part of them.

And for all those moments, I want to say thank you. Thank you for listening to me, understanding me and standing beside me when I was feeling low. ❤️

Also, I seriously need to apologise to you for what happened at Lava. My behaviour that day was completely wrong, and I know that. I genuinely regret it. I wanted to apologise to you in person, but somehow I couldn't gather the courage to do it properly.

I don't know what you think about me because of that incident, but I want you to know that I'm truly sorry.

We've also gone out with our friends, visited different shops and spent time together. Those were some genuinely happy moments for me. I enjoyed every bit of that time, and I'll always remember those memories. 😊

Now, there's one more thing I want to tell you. I know this might change the way you look at me, and I'm honestly a little scared to say it. But I can't keep it a secret anymore. I want you to know what I've been feeling all these years.

Yes, I genuinely love you.

I know that saying this might change your opinion of me. Maybe the picture you have of me might change. Maybe things between us might become different. You might even decide that you don't want to talk to me anymore or block me. I understand that possibility, and I won't blame you for it.

I know this is my feeling, and I don't expect you to feel the same way.

If I propose to you, I already have an idea of what your answer might be. I know there's a possibility that your answer will be NO. I'm not saying this because I want sympathy or because I'm expecting you to change your mind.

I just don't want to hide my feelings from you anymore.

If you ever wish to talk about this, you can approach me whenever you're comfortable. I'll never force you to discuss it, and I won't keep asking you about it. It's completely your choice.

You don't even have to give me an answer immediately. Take your time. Even if it takes one or two years, if you ever feel like talking about this, I'll be happy to listen. But please don't feel that you have to wait or that you're responsible for my feelings. Your happiness and comfort matter too.

You know, I have so many dreams. I've imagined a future where I could achieve many things, and in some of those dreams, I had someone special beside me. I even imagined doing so much for you and fulfilling your wishes.

But I know dreams don't always become reality. Maybe in another universe, things might have turned out differently. 😅❤️

And finally, there's one little thing I want to explain.

Remember the nickname I gave you? "Kitty" 🐱

Do you know why I chose that name?

Because I always wanted you to remain that childish, innocent and silly version of yourself around the people you're close to. Someone who can share everything without fear, someone who doesn't have to pretend to be different and someone who can be completely herself.

I hope you never lose that side of yourself. It's genuinely one of the things I like most about you. ❤️

There are still so many things I want to share with you, so many memories I could talk about and so many feelings I couldn't put into words. But I think this message is already long enough. 😂

I've wanted to say all these things for years, and finally, I've found the courage to write them down.

After reading this, you might see me differently. Maybe our friendship will remain the same, maybe things will change, or maybe we won't talk as much as before. I honestly don't know what will happen next.

But whatever happens, I won't blame you.

I would genuinely love to hear your opinion whenever you're comfortable sharing it. No pressure, no expectations and no force.

And one last thing...

Right now, I'm a jobless person who hasn't achieved everything he dreams of yet. I can't promise you anything today. But I hope that one day I'll become successful, achieve my dreams and become someone who can make the people around me happy.

I have so many wishes and dreams, and one of them has always been to see you achieve everything you wish for too.

If there's one thing I genuinely wish for, it's that all your dreams come true. And if I ever get the opportunity, I'd love to be someone who contributes to your happiness.

Take care of yourself, Kitty. 🐱❤️

With lots of memories, feelings and a little bit of courage,

From,
Your Jobless Person. 😅❤️
